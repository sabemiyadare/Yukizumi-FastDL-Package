----------------------------------------------
Uboa Pizza (cs_pizza_ya_san) - Hostage Rescue
----------------------------------------------
original map by takedeppo.50cal
converted to Counter-Strike by H1k0Pwn5j00 (Hiko Zukushiro)

Rogue pizza shop employees have taken five customers, and four employees hostage.

They are demanding that all of the materials needed for a whole pizza must be transferred to them
so that they can complete their own pizza and for their own enjoyment.

One of the employees had to call the police to rescue him, including the customers and the other
three employees.

Counter-Terrorists: Rescue all of the hostages and bring them either to the meat shop. Good luck.

Terrorists: Prevent all of the Counter-Terrorists from rescuing the hostages. Use all of your forces!

----------------------------------------------
There are health kits sitting in certain areas. These are really useful in case if you are wounded.

There is a assault suit near T spawn. The AK47 is located underground of the pizza shop.

Map dedicated to all Anime, Uboa, and Counter-Strike fans.
